Researched August 2026, Essonne corridor.

## What nobody is doing

Three gaps, in order of size.

**1. Tax-credit-first B2C copy at commune depth.** Vitroclair has the commune
pages but sells on "propreté impeccable". ACM has the credibility but writes for
building managers. Vesta has the SAP declaration but sells ménage. Nobody in this
corridor combines *per-service × per-commune* pages with *the arithmetic of the
50% credit shown on screen*.

**2. Bin cleaning.** `nettoyage de poubelles [commune]` has essentially no
dedicated local page anywhere in 91. It is a real household need, it is cheap to
deliver, and it is counter-seasonal to window work.

**3. Honest exclusions.** Every competitor is vague about what qualifies for the
credit. A page that states plainly that facade cleaning does *not* qualify, and
explains why, wins the featured snippet and buys trust that no amount of
"expertise reconnue" can. It also happens to be the legally required position.

## What Vesta proves

Vesta displays its declaration number, its intervention mode (prestataire), and
an explicit "no travel fees, no admin fees, no file fees" line. That is exactly
the trust architecture this site needs. Copy the structure, not the words.

Vesta is also the reason blocker #1 in `STATUS.md` is urgent: a competitor two
towns over is doing the compliant version of the same claim.

## Competitive posture for this build

Do not fight JBR, Anthes, or ACM on B2B contracts. The client has one operator
and no references.

Fight for the household searching on a Saturday morning, in twelve named towns,
with a page that answers their actual question and halves the price in front of
them.
