# 01 — Market and ideal customer

## Geography

Service area as claimed today is inconsistent across sources.

| Source | Claim |
|---|---|
| Client flyer / brief | Linas, Arpajon, Étampes — depts 77, 91, 94 |
| Live IONOS site | Essonne (91) and Val-de-Marne (94) |
| Reality of the three named towns | All three are 91 |

Linas, Arpajon and Étampes sit on a single north-south axis along the N20 / RER C
corridor. Seine-et-Marne (77) and Val-de-Marne (94) are 40–70 minutes away and
share no border with Étampes. **Targeting them dilutes every local signal.**

Recommendation: build for 91 only. Twelve communes on the corridor. Expand later
if the client actually takes jobs outside it.

### The twelve

Linas, Montlhéry, Arpajon, Saint-Germain-lès-Arpajon, La Norville, Ollainville,
Bruyères-le-Châtel, Égly, Brétigny-sur-Orge, Marcoussis, Étampes, Étréchy.

Chosen for: contiguity along the N20, high owner-occupier housing share, and
detached houses with real glazing surface. These are the households that buy
window cleaning, not apartment renters.

## Primary ICP — the eligible homeowner

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Housing | Detached or semi-detached house, owner-occupier |
| Glazing | Bay windows, a veranda, or roof windows they cannot safely reach |
| Household | Dual income, 35–65, time-poor rather than cash-poor |
| Tax position | Pays income tax, so the credit is worth its full face value |
| Trigger | Spring, before a family event, after building work, or after a storm |
| Search behaviour | `nettoyage vitres [their town]`, on mobile, weekend morning |
| Objection | "Is this actually worth it versus doing it myself" |
| What flips them | Seeing €90 become €45 on screen, with the mechanism explained |

## Secondary ICP — the small business

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Shopfront, small office, medical practice, restaurant |
| Need | Vitrine cleaning on a schedule, not one-off |
| Value | Lower ticket, higher frequency, full margin, no cooperative commission |
| Search behaviour | `nettoyage vitrine [town]`, `société de nettoyage [town]` |
| What flips them | A named contact and a fast quote, not a price list |

## Third ICP — the syndic / copropriété

Not targeted in v1. Worth noting because it is the highest-value segment and the
existing local players (JBR, Anthes, ACM) all live there. Entering it needs
insurance documentation and references the client does not yet have.

## Demand shape

Window cleaning demand is **seasonal and event-driven**, not steady. Peaks in
March–May and September–October. This matters for two reasons:

1. The site must rank before the spring peak, not during it. Indexing a new
   domain takes weeks.
2. Bin cleaning and housekeeping are counter-seasonal filler. They belong on the
   site partly to smooth the calendar, not only to catch search volume.

## Volume expectation, stated honestly

These are low-volume queries. `nettoyage vitres Arpajon` is not a thousand
searches a month. It is closer to a handful.

That is the point. A handful of searches with near-total purchase intent, in a
market where the top result is a directory page rather than a business, is worth
more than a high-volume term the client can never win. Seventy-two pages each
catching a trickle produce a steady flow, and the marginal cost of page 73 is
close to zero.

**Do not oversell traffic numbers to the client.** Sell position and intent.
